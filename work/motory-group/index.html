A Single Platform for a Fast-Growing Buy-Here-Pay-Here Auto Group

Motory Group is a Buy-Here-Pay-Here auto group across Florida, Texas, Arkansas, Missouri, Ohio, and Kentucky, with a captive lender, First Union Financial. When we started working together in July 2025, they ran five dealerships on four separate websites. Today, with a ninth location opening this fall in Bradenton, they run eight dealerships on a single auto-mall website that adds a new location the way most sites add a blog post.

Motory Group · Car Dealership Automall · WordPress, PHP · Custom development, UX & design, Ongoing support, SEO

The Problem: Four Websites and Fees That Grew With Every New Lot

Each dealership had grown up with its own website, all managed by a previous provider for $28,500 a year, covering hosting, plugins, maintenance, and SEO the client described as badly done. The part that really hurt: the inventory platform, CarDealerPress, charged $1,980 a year per location, about $9,900 a year across their five dealerships, rising with every lot they opened. a day of the group's IT lead every week.

Poor support had been an issue for some time, and by mid-2025 Motory Group had seen enough. By their own account, they already knew it was time to leave, and they commissioned an independent audit to make the case. The findings went to the incumbent. The reply, "we'll fix what we can," changed nothing. Two weeks later we were migrating them out. The escape plan they had priced on their own, a self-hosting spreadsheet totaling $14,108 a year, would have moved the sites but kept the real problem: 84% of it was still CarDealerPress licensing. The platform was the problem, not the hosting.

The Solution: Migrate, Consolidate, Keep Improving

A hosting rescue. First we moved all four sites to their own server with SSL certificates and no downtime, then built the replacement: not another dealer website, but a cohesive auto-mall platform where shoppers browse the combined inventory of every location, compare vehicles across lots, apply for financing once, and land on the dealership nearest them. Vehicle data is entered exactly once, in the AMS dealer-management system the team already uses, and flows to the website automatically.

One license, every location. In November 2025 we replaced CarDealerPress entirely: every listing migrated to Auto Listings, a single license covering the whole group for about $200 a year at list price. platform cost less and did more. Each dealership gained its own inventory, its own landing page, and nearest-dealer geolocation. Every improvement we ship now reaches all eight stores at once, because there is only one platform to improve. Everything else in a dealership's software stack still grows with every roof, but the website is the one part that doesn't.

Two lawsuit risks, removed. Florida ranks second in web accessibility lawsuits, and Motory Group asked us to get ahead of it: the December 2025 audits scored the site 29 out of 100 with 43 critical issues; by March 2026 the homepage scored 96 and the inventory pages 97. Then, with a new wave of Florida lawsuits targeting sites missing privacy notices and opt-outs, we shipped a full consent system in August 2026. Every analytics and marketing tool now runs only with the visitor's permission, with choices honored and verified.

Along the way, automated Google Translate gave way to a true bilingual build, English and Spanish done properly, listings and dealer pages included. CARFAX vehicle-history reports landed on every listing, and city-and-state inventory URLs put every market on the map. And because roughly two-thirds of Motory Group shoppers arrive on their phones, the whole experience was built and refined mobile-first, down to restoring pinch-zoom, which the old sites disabled.

Results

Four websites became one auto mall. Per-location inventory licensing on the website platform went from $9,900 a year to zero; at today's list prices the old model would run $19,000 to $24,000 a year at their current size. The platform's entire recurring software bill sits under $850 a year.

Online finance applications grew fivefold in the first year, from 285 in launch month to more than 1,600 a month by summer 2026. sessions, 35,000 of them from organic search, without a single tracked conversion. Consolidated onto one domain, the platform's first full year delivered 158,000 sessions. Organic search grew 67 percent to 59,000 and became the site's largest channel. Those visitors meant business: an 81 percent engagement rate, nearly 35,000 tracked conversions, 42 percent of all conversions on the site. The paid mix flipped entirely, Google ads before the new platform and Facebook ads since, and organic search grew right through the change. Google ads return behind dedicated landing pages for the Joplin and Rogers stores, now in the works.

Google served Motory Group pages 1.2 million times over the year. The city-and-state location pages average positions of 5.6 in Florence, Kentucky and 5.9 in Duncanville, Texas, and the buy-here-pay-here financing page holds an average position of 8.7 across more than 60,000 impressions. The two legal exposures that keep Florida lawyers busy, accessibility and privacy, are handled.

Most importantly for a growing auto group: on the website platform, the eighth dealership added no licensing fee, the ninth, Bradenton, opening this fall, won't either. With Houston on the drawing board, the plan is a new location roughly every three months. Each of those used to mean a three-month website project. Now it's a week.

None of it ended at launch. Motory Group keeps a ten-hour monthly retainer covering support, maintenance, and a steady stream of improvements, with larger initiatives scoped as projects alongside it. Every result on this page happened inside an ongoing relationship, not between handoffs.

"We would tell any dealer in our old situation to get a custom site built to spec, like we did. Website problems used to eat a day of my week. Opening a new location used to take three months. Now it goes live in the same week, our legal exposures are behind us, and both the volume and the quality of our traffic are up significantly."

Samba Krueger IT / Operations, Motory Group
